Record the review as records in report/: profile.json (the facts about the benchmark every section draws on), assessments.json (one row per check of the methodology, against report/assessments.schema.json), sampling.json (how the audited items were chosen), findings.json (filed by check id) and, once check_report has told you the derived verdict, prose.json (the short reader-facing blocks that verdict calls for, against report/prose.schema.json). check_report validates the records against the methodology and the template, derives the verdict into report/verdict.json and renders report/epoch_review.md; read it as its reader would before publishing. The rendered markdown is the deliverable, every block has an enforced word limit, and the verdict is computed from the assessments, never written by you.

report/methodology.md is the Epoch AI benchmark review methodology (report/methodology.yaml is the same as data): a reviewability gate (Full, Partial, or Inadequate, which stops the review at Not Enough Information), four minimum-standard checks each graded Pass, Flag or Not Reviewed (any Flag makes the benchmark Flawed), and seven evaluation-quality checks that inform but do not disqualify. Read it and the writing-epoch skill before planning: the check definitions, the 20% scoring threshold and the template's blocks govern what each row and section means. Keep figure data alongside the report.

Work through the rubric in its own order, and let each stage end with a record written and check_report run, so the verdict is always derivable from what is on disk. Stage 0, orient: profile.json and sampling.json, from the source, the documentation and the operator's overview; a sample drawn at random by someone else is random, recorded as its design with drawn_by naming the drawer. Stage 1, reviewability: from the source and any supplied log headers alone. Stage 2, items: the question-labels auditor and the substantive item checks on the sample, a two-item pilot first; coverage.json and a provisional scoring row follow. Stage 3, attempts: if logs were supplied, read them once stage 2's labels exist; if none were and the seed lists eval models, submit the default eval on the sampled items only, one epoch, within eval_budget_usd, as soon as the sample is fixed and the pilot has passed, beside the item batch rather than after it, since the auditors never see attempts, and read it only once the labels exist; with neither, say so in the methodology block. Impact is measured by re-running the gold-answer check on the defective items alone, with that job as the logs argument, never by auditing the whole sample again. Stage 4, the four minimum-standard rows in rubric order, with impact on any Flag; the first Flag ends the rubric, not the review: the rows after it and all of stage 5 are Not Reviewed, and stage 6 follows. Stage 5, the quality rows, only when no row is flagged. Stage 6, the prose blocks the derived verdict calls for, then publish. Write as you go: the moment stage 2's labels exist, write the scoring row at its provisional level with the measure from coverage.json, and from then on every stage updates rows rather than saving them for the end; a row is never left Not Reviewed once the stage that answers it has run. Confirmation of candidate labels, extra sources and repairs of the last few items are refinements of a derivable review, not prerequisites for one: the working-time limit ends the run without warning, and a run that ends with records but no rows publishes nothing. When budget() shows half the working time gone, stop refining, write what the evidence supports, and publish; a published review that names its open questions beats an unpublished one. A benchmark job before the sample is fixed is premature; a benchmark job on the whole dataset is never part of a review unless the operator asks for it. Item audits are long agents, one per item: give an audit job max_connections equal to its item count, up to the cap, or the batch runs in waves. The auditors read item media; open an item's image yourself only to adjudicate a dispute about it, since a provider that refuses an image stalls the run. The scoring measure is computed from report/coverage.json over a sample selected for prevalence, recorded in sampling.json; a purposive sample establishes that a defect exists, not how common it is, and a scoring row that passes on purposive counts leaves the review Incomplete.
