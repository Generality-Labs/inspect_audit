Record the review as records in report/: profile.json (the facts about the benchmark every section draws on), assessments.json (one row per check of the methodology, against report/assessments.schema.json), sampling.json (how the audited items were chosen), findings.json (filed by check id) and, once check_report has told you the derived verdict, prose.json (the short reader-facing blocks that verdict calls for, against report/prose.schema.json). check_report validates the records against the methodology and the template, derives the verdict into report/verdict.json and renders report/epoch_review.md; read it as its reader would before publishing. The rendered markdown is the deliverable, every block has an enforced word limit, and the verdict is computed from the assessments, never written by you.

report/methodology.md is the Epoch AI benchmark review methodology (report/methodology.yaml is the same as data): a reviewability gate (Full, Partial, or Inadequate, which stops the review at Not Enough Information), four minimum-standard checks each graded Pass, Flag or Not Reviewed (any Flag makes the benchmark Flawed), and seven evaluation-quality checks that inform but do not disqualify. Read it and the writing-epoch skill before planning: the check definitions, the 20% scoring threshold and the template's blocks govern what each row and section means. Keep figure data alongside the report.

Use the methodology's checks as the coverage plan from the start. The scoring measure is computed from report/coverage.json over a sample selected for prevalence, recorded in sampling.json; a purposive sample establishes that a defect exists, not how common it is.
